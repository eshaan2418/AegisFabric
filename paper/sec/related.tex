\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{WAN traffic engineering.}
B4~\citep{b4} and SWAN~\citep{swan} showed that centralised TE over a private inter-datacenter WAN can run
links at high utilisation by allocating measured or declared demand across paths. Espresso~\citep{espresso}
moved BGP peering into software for application-aware routing at Google's edge. Our \emph{independent}
baseline is a deliberately simple member of this family: a min-max-utilisation MILP over measured
per-prefix demand. We ask what happens when the TE objective is not the job's objective, and when the
controller may also move the job. We do not claim our TE matches the sophistication of those systems.

\paragraph{Parallelism and placement.}
Megatron-LM~\citep{megatron} composes tensor, pipeline and data parallelism and shows that poor
combinations cost throughput even on fast interconnects. FSDP~\citep{fsdp} shards parameters, gradients
and optimiser state. DiLoCo~\citep{diloco} attacks the WAN problem algorithmically by communicating much less
often. That is complementary to our approach, which keeps the synchronous algorithm and changes where
ranks run and how their bytes are routed.

\paragraph{Checkpointing.}
CheckFreq~\citep{checkfreq} pipelines snapshot and persist and tunes frequency online. Check-N-Run~\citep{checknrun}
makes checkpoints incremental and quantized, and Gemini~\citep{gemini} checkpoints to CPU memory and
explicitly schedules checkpoint traffic so that it does not interfere with training traffic. That
interference is the same interaction our cost model represents when it charges ring edges that share a
link with a flush. Our checkpoint engine (content-addressed chunks, write-ahead log, verified recovery)
borrows standard storage-system techniques. The novelty claimed here is only in treating the checkpoint
\emph{destination and path} as part of the joint decision.

\paragraph{Real-time media.}
Salsify~\citep{salsify} couples the video codec and the transport in one control loop. Our media extension
asks a similar ``joint versus independent'' question, with the inference scheduler as a third loop.

\paragraph{Privacy and post-training.}
We use DP-SGD~\citep{dpsgd} through Opacus~\citep{opacus}, loss-threshold membership inference in the
spirit of Shokri et al.~\citep{shokri}, FedAvg~\citep{fedavg}, DPO~\citep{dpo} and GRPO~\citep{deepseekmath}. All of
these run at toy scale, as sanity-checked components of the platform rather than as contributions.

\paragraph{Formal methods.}
We model-check the checkpoint commit/recovery and certificate-rotation protocols in TLA+~\citep{tla}
with TLC.
